# TinyCreature

TinyCreature is the Black & White terrain running on the Tang core.
Open TinyCreature from the start menu and choose Start on its Launch
page. The page says which of the core, player and Celtic scene is
missing when the bundle has not been installed.

The scene uses your own Black & White data. TinyTang does not include
that game data; the TinyCreature repository builds it from your copy.

## Controls

- Middle-drag turns and tilts around the land under the hand.
- Left-drag moves across the land.
- The wheel moves in and out.
- F12 or Escape ends the scene and returns to TinyDesk.

F12 ends this app instead of showing TinyDesk over it. The
TinyCreature core has no desktop layer to draw over its picture, so
the launcher restores the desktop core when the player exits.
